Getting started

Three steps to ready

1

Log in

Access ClickUp at app.clickup.com with your Good Growth email. Accept the workspace invite if you haven't already.

2

Take the tour

Work through the modules below in order. Start with Workspace Overview — it gives everything else context.

3

Start working

Open your personal to-do list and try creating your first task. There's no staging environment — just do it.
