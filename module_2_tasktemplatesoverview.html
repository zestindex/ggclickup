What are task templates?

What they do

When a Delivery Manager selects a template on an Experience task, a standard set of subtasks is automatically created — covering every stage from proposal through to results. This ensures consistent delivery structure across all client work.

Why we have different ones

Not all experiences are the same. A quick demo has different needs to a complex enterprise programme. Templates are sized to the work — so the structure is always appropriate, never excessive or insufficient.

The three mandatory milestones — present in every template

Milestone 1
🏁 Client Sign-Off
After the proposal / brief stage. Nothing moves to Design or Build until this is complete.
Milestone 2
🏁 Client QA
After internal QA is signed off. Opens the client review stage. Nothing goes to client until this gate is reached.
Milestone 3
🏁 Ready for Live
After all QA and approvals are complete. Nothing launches until this milestone is marked complete.

Task types used across all templates

Discovery
Research, ideation, and scoping work. Understanding the problem before committing to a solution.
Design
Speccing and designing the experience — brief, acceptance criteria, visual design, and build planning.
Build
Development and implementation work, including fixing issues identified in QA.
Decide
QA, review, analysis, and results — assessing quality and measuring outcomes.
Milestone
Formal gate points. Must be marked complete before the next stage can begin.

How to select a template

1
Fill in the Experience detailsExperience Code, Client, T-Shirt Size, Credit Size.
2
Select a template from the Subtask Template dropdownThis triggers automatic subtask creation. The structure is created immediately — you don't need to tick Details Complete first.
3
Review the subtasks and assign ownersSubtasks are created unassigned. Assign the relevant team members before the experience moves into active delivery.
4
Tick Experience Details CompleteThis triggers field stamping — Experience Code, Client, sizing fields, and time estimates are pushed down to the relevant subtasks automatically.
